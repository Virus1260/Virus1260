## `◈` Engineering Philosophy

```
"Every kilogram of steel placed under pressure is a decision backed by calculation.
 Every calculation automated is a decision made consistently, infinitely."

```

I engineer static equipment that operates at the edge of material science — from cryogenic vaporizers at **−212 °C** to reactor internals at **+1,200 °C**, across corrosion-resistant exotics like Super Duplex, Hastelloy, and Titanium. My work has passed client review at **ARAMCO**, **KNPC**, **ExxonMobil**, **ADNOC**, **Reliance**, **Air Products**, and **Linde** — with **zero non-conformance reports**.

Beyond hardware, I build the software that replaces repetitive engineering labour. My in-house **Python/AutoCAD surface-development engine** cut drafting from **32 hours down to 4 hours per equipment** — a gain that compounds across every project the team delivers.
